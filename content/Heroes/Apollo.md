## Character Tips
---
- **!! Turn on Hero Specific Keybinds and rebind Flawless Advance to Right Click !!**
	- This leaves your left hand completely open for all movement directions when stabbing
	- Zoom isn't important on Apollo anyways since his projectiles literally disappear at 25m
- **Flawless Advance** is the ability most player struggle with the most when picking up Apollo as it's most of your damage but also weird to control
	- The Ability has 2 main bursts of movement which allow you to adjust for the enemies movement
	- Holding a movement key when pressing the ability key gives you a burst in a direction
	- The second burst of movement goes in the direction you are looking when releasing the ability key. This can allow you to push yourself around corners and can hit enemies who aren't hugging the wall
	- Getting used to the way Flawless Advance behaves is crucial for learning the character and I recommend sitting in practice tool for a few minutes just practicing weird stabs around corners
- Similarly to Flawless Advance **Itani Lo San** has some weird movement quirks
	- Imagine that Apollo has a massive sphere around him during the dash. The dash will continue as far as it can without **fully** colliding with a wall. Obviously any enemy colliding with this sphere also gets affected by the damage
	- Here is an example of the bullshit you can do with this Ability (black trail is where the dash goes)
	- ![[Pasted image 20261013173629.png|418]]
	- Again, spend some time in practice tool to get a feel for it and how to properly curve it
- **Riposte** is one of his most important cooldowns and it's important to know what it procs off of
	- As far as I know all DOT effects do not proc riposte
	- NPCs from heroes like Graves skulls or McGinnis turrets do proc Riposte
	- Riposte's dash range is stupidly far and can bring a Grey Talon or Vindicta out the Sky or used to escape if someone is deep in your backline
	- It's almost guaranteed someone will shoot you if you are actively fighting, don't think about it too much when to use it just to guarantee the Riposte
	- Tier 2 duration increase guarantees a heavy melee, Debuff Reducer will let them parry you
	- Amazing to shut down long channelled ults like Haze, Bebop, or McGinnis
- **Disengaging Sigil** will deal damage in an AOE in the direction you are looking as soon as the jump happens, the original Sigil on cast doesn't actually dictate where the AOE gets cast
	- Keep tracking the enemy throughout the entire Sigil cast time to guarantee it hits
	- The AOE will go through walls if they are thin enough
- You can sneak a light melee into **Disengaging Sigil** if you spam melee right as you backflip
- **Disengaging Sigil** can be used to increase wall jump velocity
	- Cast Sigil while being close to Wall
	- Hold S
	- Try to time your jump for when the backflip would happen
	- Can technically use scroll wheel jump to guarantee, but will consume 1 stamina because you double jump directly after
	- The way to figure out if you are doing it correctly is trying it on this spot in the practice tool

## General Gameplan
---
- Your lane is incredibly strong with stupid amounts of poke with your **Disengaging Sigil**
	- **Flawless Advance** T1 and Melee Lifesteal are your main sustain tools
- Remember to not heavy melee after a Riposte until you have T2
- Apollo is strongest around the 15-30 minute mark and you should be utilizing any tempo you have out of lane as much as possible in midgame
- You are able to comfortably 1v1 anyone on the map in midgame assuming you hit your spells
- Always keep track of who has **Slowing Hex** and how dangerous they are during that 3 second window where you turn into a melee minion
- Lategame you are looking to annoy the backline and maybe clean up kills with Itani Lo San, they will probably have too much health for you to comfortably kill them with one ability cycle
- Your lategame sustain mainly comes from Riposte heavy melee 

### Fights:
- Always keep track of what needs to be shut down in fights and look for big Ripostes
	- Think of yourself as a Knockdown on an 11 second cd
	- Vindicta, GT, Bebop Ult, McGinnis Ult, Haze Ult
- Apollo dosen't have a Combo I could write down just land ur damage on as many people as possible innit
- Your survivability without CDs is horrendous and you should always have an idea on how deep you can go and how you can leave after using your spells


## Counter Tips
---
- **Slowing Hex Slowing Hex Slowing Hex Slowing Hex Slowing Hex Slowing Hex**
	- Literally stops all abilities except Riposte from working and guts his damage
	- Buying multiple and spacing them out will fuck over his Dispel aswell
	- A lot of Apollos will panic and start meleeing while slowing hexed meaning free parries 
- **Debuff Reducer** stops him from guaranteeing a Heavy Melee after stun and allows you to parry
	- If he buys Duration Extender, which almost nobody does, you can't parry anymore
- **Counterspell** works on his ult and is pretty easy to hit since it's so telegraphed
	- Obviously very easy to hit on sigil and stab aswell when ur low but don't waste the cd on those abilties if ur full hp
- **Rebuttal** can work by parrying nothing, making them heavy melee twice and parrying the second melee
	- Depends on the skill level of the Apollo and if he's tracking who has rebuttal and who doesn't
