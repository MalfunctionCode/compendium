### Character Tips
---
- Pocket is a character who fights backwards
	- You dive into the backline, deal your damage and then work your way back to your team's frontline
	- His pre fight mobility comes from Items (see [[Pocket#Itemization]])
- Pocket starts with -15% Spirit Resist, meaning certain spirit heavy lanes can be horrible for you
	- Most difficult lane matchups: Apollo, Mina, Holliday, GT 
	- There are ways to itemize against this early (see [[Pocket#Itemization]])
- Most of your damage actually lies in the Barrage damage amp and a big part of learning Pocket is learning how to hit your Barrages consistently 
	- Barrage is a slow projectile and should never be used horizontally
	- Always Barrage from above someone, to make the distance towards the target shorter
	- Barrage damage amp applies retroactively to affliction so don't feel like you always have to barrage before going in
- While waiting for cloak to fly, you can obviously Satchel to wait for the cloak to fly further
	- If you tp to cloak while in Satchel, the Satchel explosion will happen at the cloak destination which is suboptimal
	- Instead, try learning to jump out of Satchel and instantly tp afterwards, to deal a bit more damage 
	- This has some risk involved, and you should always consider immediate cc threats (curses, silences) and if the Satchel damage would be meaningful right now 
- Always consider the enemy team comp and item state before going into fights to hit good afflictions
	- The healing denial of Affliction can completely fuck over frontlines and it might be more valuable to ult a fed Victor in the frontline than going for a risky backline affliction which might miss a slippery target
	- Always keep an eye out for who has DBs and figure out what the optimal play based on that is. Pulling a support into an unfavorable position to DB a priority target can win fights
	- Affliction has a wind-up time and learning to time the actual affliction hit with your Majestic Leap dive is very important. Depending on height you can cast Affliciton before dropping and it will hit as soon as you land 

### General Gameplan
---
- Your early-midgame actually is actually rather weak and you should be focusing on macroing and clearing camps till you hit your big spikes
	- You can effectively clear two lanes at the same time by throwing a cloak to the other lane, satcheling the wave and then tping
	- Keep in mind you get less (or no) money from doing this, so consider how much time you have to get back to the other lane or even how important prio on that lane is etc etc.
- Late-game you should be at as many big fights as possible by using your Majestic Leap and Cloak to be in the right place
	- You'd think Trophy would be good on Pocket, but since his early is so slow it's really suboptimal and you won't be in all the fights in early game to properly stack it
- As mentioned above, pick your Affliction targets and try to dive and full combo them, after that you are just hitting whoever you can with your gun till you get a cloak + satchel combo back. Most of your value comes out of that initial burst.
- **General Combo: Leap -> Barrage -> Leap Drop -> Affliction -> Cloak (Backwards through enemy) -> Satchel -> Leave Satchel -> Cloak TP** 
### Itemization
---
Similarly to [[Celeste]], Pocket dosen't really need flat spirit due to his Barrage amp. Instead range, duration and cooldown are insanely valuable stats on him increasing his uptime, survivability and opportunities. Battle Vest and gun spike are also really strong early invests which can help him finish up kills in early fights where his ability damage is still lacking.

Pockets negative starting spirit resist mean that he has some different early build paths depending on the enemy composition and lane:
- **Enchanters Emblem** can directly replace Battle Vest and is more slot efficient but lowers your early kill power significantly 
- **Mystic Vulnerability** and **Cold Front** together add up to 14% Spirit Resist and work very well on Pocket. Additionally the Mystiv Vuln shred can help into tanky comps and Cold Front can really help bringing your burst potential online earlier.  This build path is way more slot hungry though and both Items will have to be sold later to accommodate for more important Items. What I'd also do when using this path is skip out on Tankbuster until way later, since you have 4.8k Spirit already and a pre 25 minute Tankbuster dosen't have much value.

Pocket also has some absolutely core active items:
- **Dispel Magic** is a crucial item on Pocket that should be bought even if the enemy team has very few damage over time effects. Slowing Hex stops you from being able to tp to your cloak and good players will slowing hex you out of satchel, making your cloak expire and leaving you stranded in the enemy team. Late game with Superior Duration and Lvl 3 satchel this is less of an issue, but the 10% Spirit Resist and on demand heal is still valuable. 
- **Majestic Leap/Warp Stone** are his main movement items and picked based on whatever you prefer. Some players prefer Warp Stone for allowing you to jumpscare afflict them, instead of heavily telegraphing your engage with Majestic Leap. However, Majestic Leap allows you more angles of attack and also just in general makes you quicker to follow up on engages.  
	- Personally I learned Pocket back when Majestic Leap was stronger and most of my muscle memory works around Majestic Leap. I'm a Majestic Leap truther. 

### Counter Tips
---
- **Divine Barrier Divine Barrier Divine Barrier Divine Barrier Divine Barrier Divine Barrier **
	- Cleanses Affliction on a Target or urself
	- If you are on a M1 Carry and have suboptimal supports consider buying it instead of Dispel, as it has the same cooldown as Dispel when you use it on urself. 
- As mentioned earlier, **Slowing Hex** stops Pocket from Tping to cloak and can lead to a free kill if you time it with the cloak timing out
- **Silencer** not only reduces all his damage by 25% but also just completely fucks his combo if you get the buildup effect at the right time (when he is flying in the air with maj leap for example)
- If the Pocket has Cheat Death using **Curse** while Cheat Death is active just instantly kills him
