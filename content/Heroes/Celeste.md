## Character Tips
---
- Bullets Ricochet off of Walls/Players and have stronger tracking after a Bounce
	- Generally should be aiming around the ground close to enemies to guarantee hits
	- Can secure 2 souls/damage multiple enemies if they are lined up behind each other
- A lot of the characters survivability comes from being slippery and weaving in and out of fights
	- Reduced Gravity and increased air acceleration help with this
	- Bind jump to scroll down to bhop consistently while out of stamina
	- You can bhop with stam by using spacebar (more forgiving than most source games)
- Radiant Daggers ignores all terrain vertically
	- Very Important to learn the location of midboss pit so you can hit it from blue lane when coming in for contests
- With Witchmail be mindful of ability usage, as it can only refund cooldowns if the ability is actually on cooldown
	- Once reaching 6 stacks on Daggers refrain from using it unless you are refreshing
	- Only use Light Eater when you really have to deal gun damage to someone/are low
	- You want as many Witchmail procs on shield/ball as possible

## General Gameplan
---
- You are a position 1 character, your lane is weak and you only really fully come online once you have Witchmail 
- Play very safe in lane and just poke by procing Mystic Burst on Daggers
- Solo lane in midgame and eat as much farm as possible to get your key Items
- Box Routes are even stronger on celeste since you have faster movement 

### Fights:
- Try to get 6 Stacks on Daggers before using Ball/commiting to the fight fully
- Light Eater should be on ball targets so you can stay alive during fights
- Your main damage in team fights is ball
	- Your gun and Light Eater damage is mainly for cleaning up after ball has done it's thing
	- Once you hit Spellslinger and max Daggers in late game your gun damage becomes more important

## Itemization
---
The main idea behind celeste itemization is that you don't want to invest heavily into straight spirit damage, since most of your damage comes from the spirit amp on your 3. Therefore most of the investment goes into util items and survivability, since she really thrives in extended fights. As mentioned in the [[Celeste#Character Tips]], your Witchmail should proc on ball and shield as much as possible to help u survive while u tick them down.

After getting your Core 6ks (Witchmail, Escalating, Plated) you can opt into a few different late Items:
- Spellslinger is an obvious choice after getting tier 3 Daggers, since the reload speed is very necesarry on her small mag. My general buy, but I opt for refresher instead into hard targets.
- Refresher can absolutely fuck up teamfights, as finding a safe spot for one person is hard enough. As mentioned above I opt for this if I struggle hitting enemies on the enemy team (vindicta, vyper, mina...)
- Transcendent Cooldown is a tempting upgrade, and definetley has merit on Celeste as it reduces Torment Pulse Intervals, Escalating Exposure buildup, and even allows for 25% faster Witchmail procs. However I think it should be bought after one of the 6ks mentioned above (especially when it's a refresher game), since the spikes of Spellslinger or Refresher are just too strong. **DO BUY IF YOU ARE SAVING 6K BUT ABOUT TO LOSE THE GAME**
## Counter Tips
--- 
- Slowing Hex guts her mobility due to increased gravity scaling and Knockdown can stop her from running away  
- Silencer is a free 25% damage reduction on her entire kit and should be first bought on any gun character into her
- Antiheal is insanely important against her and should be bought as soon as she gets radiant regen
- Disarming Hex ruins her non ball damage 
- Ball can't be cleansed by Divine Barrier, Counterspelled or removed in any way
- If you hear Ball being cast, just try to isolate as best as you can
