## Character Tips
---
- Your **Alt-Fire** is your main damage tool for enemy heroes and it's important to learn the projectile's behaviour
	- The entire AOE procs effects like Mystic/Resto Shot
	- It should be woven in between abilities as it has an internal cooldown which isn't modifiable by fire rate (?)
	- After using a full combo use the alt fire as your main damage by shooting it on the ground next to them (mixing in light melees with this is very effective)
	- Left click technically has more dps, but is very difficult to use and I usually only use it on farm and towers
- **Power Slash** doesn't linearly scale it's damage, instead it scales in increments denoted on the reticle
	- ![[Pasted image 20261011013737.png|491]]
	- Therefore you should try to never press left click to confirm a slash, unless they will definitely break LOS
- **Power Slash** also has a few important Quirks
	- **!! RIGHT HAND PEAKS ARE KING !!** The actual damage comes from the right of your model meaning you can be completely around a corner and still hit someone
	- ![[Pasted image 20261013182013.png|547]]
	- You can very slightly slide around by holding movement keys while charging
	- If you roll and click the ability you charge while conserving the roll momentum
	- Crouching off of ziplines and using the ability quickly afterwards lets you charge while floating through the air with the zipline momentum
- Yamato inherently is an amazing laner against m1 carries and you should try to be in a lane against one
	- Fire Rate slow on your **Crimson Slash** makes every early trade winning assuming you hit it
	- Max **Power Slash** spike hits incredibly hard into low hp pool carries
	- **Flying Slash** helps sticking to them and can follow their escape tools (Wraith, Vindicta etc.)
- Spirit from **Flying Slash** should be used on your Abilities if possible so make sure to use ur abilities as quickly as possible
	- Important Note: You get the Spirit as soon as your Grapple connects, not on doing the melee damage
- **Flying Slash** can be cancelled mid flight by pressing Space
	- Playing around with cancel timings for different momentum in different directions 
	- It's pretty easy to get a feel for this though once you understand it 

## General Gameplan
---
- You are a lane bully, play like one
- Alt-Fire the enemies as much as you can and try to take trades with a Crimson Slash
- Once you have Melee Lifesteal your full **2 -> Alt -> 3** combo heals you about 200 health, which can be crucial to turn early fights
- Try to get sneaky Power Slashes on right hand peaks (works especially well when they are farming under Tower)
- Midgame you should be on Blue, ready to rotate to any fights if possible and play aggressively in their jungle with a teammate
	- You can also play side and 1v1 carries trying to scale, assuming they aren't way up on you in souls and depending on the hero
- Late Game you are hounding down the enemy carries in fights but also technically have the potential to splitpush a walker
	- It's not as fast as other characters, but you have better tools to win 1v1s if they send someone which has it's own advantages and dissadvantages
### Fights:
- Figure out before fights which targets you should be focusing and take mental note of what items and abilities they could have to counter you (Dispel, Rebuttal, Counterspell, Silences, ...)
- General Full Combo: **2 -> 3 -> 1 -> 4 -> (3) -> 2 -> 1** 
	- Between every ability you should learn to get a right click in to maximize damage
	- Crimson Slash is used directly after ult since you can get it out frame one if you spam it during ult, but if nobody is close then obviously hold it till after you've used Flying Slash
- Don't be afraid to use ult first to contest midbosses as it makes you immune to parries 
	- Depending on enemy comp you either want to parry or punch
	- If they have fight turning CC (Paige, Rem, Billy, Solomon) then you should opt for parrying, as they will go for melees because the CC should prevent anyone trying to Parry
	- If they've used it while ur ult is casting or just in general don't have any big cc rather opt for punching to guarantee one, as your teammates will probably fly in and parry

## Itemization
---
Despite the characters spirit scalings, current builds generally opt into building more gun (melee) damage and playing her as like a frontliner who can stick to a target. The idea is that with recharging rush and express shot you can constantly cast grapples and hit alt fires while chasing. Her extra melee damage on her 3 also means getting a heavy melee can really chunk them, but it's obviously attached to the parry mindgame. 

The reason I prefer to buy spirit on her is because:
- It's generally more tanky (more spirit to scale radiant regen and crimson slash)
- Opting for max power slash gives you insane amounts of midgame tempo and lets you play like a pseudo drifter easily 1v1ing the enemy carries in midgame
- She still has the heavy chase utility of Gun Yam by maxxing 2 second, buying lifestrike for the slow and still having the option of recharging rush available based on game. 
- Overall the build trades a bit of damage for more consistency opposed to melee Yamato(can't be parried/have to waste Counterspell) and tankiness

**General Itemization Notes:**
- Util items like Disarming Hex or Silence Wave can work really well on her to help her 1v1 potential
- Recharging Rush is an amazing Item if their team has a lot of mobility and procs off of a Flying Slash + Alt-fire combo
- Return Fire works insanely well on this character and can singlehandedly win you 1v1s
- You generally don't want to opt for firerate slow on her, since crimson slash has very good firerate slow already. Juggernaut is not a horrible buy though especially into Viscous or Billy due to the melee resist. 

## Counter Tips
---
- Dispel into Gun Yamato, Counterspell into Spirit Yamato
	- Flying Slash can be dispelled as soon as the grapple connects to ur player
		- Make sure to break los after dispelling and in general as much as possible when being chased
	- Counterspell can completely negate her Power Slash damage 
- Rebuttal very good since a lot of yamatos will become melee heavy especially in lane
- Antiheal and Shred mainly fuck her over, as she doesn't stack much hp and mainly gets her survivability from healing and Resists
